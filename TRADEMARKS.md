Last updated: August 24, 2026

This policy explains how the AYTHER names, logos, and other brand identifiers
may be used. Its purpose is to protect users from confusion while allowing the
community to discuss, support, and build with AYTHER's open-source software.

Apache License 2.0 or any other software license applicable to an AYTHER
repository do not include a license to use AYTHER trademarks except as expressly
permitted by this policy or by applicable law.

The protected AYTHER brand identifiers include:

- **AYTHER Engine™**;
- **AYTHER Runtime™**;

The ™ symbol indicates a claim of trademark rights. It does not represent that a
mark is registered. The ® symbol must not be used for an AYTHER mark unless and
until the relevant mark has been registered and the owner expressly authorizes
that usage.

## 3. Uses that do not require permission

You may use the AYTHER word marks without prior written permission when the use
is accurate, limited to what is reasonably necessary, and does not imply
sponsorship, endorsement, affiliation, or official status.

Permitted uses include:

- truthfully referring to AYTHER or an official AYTHER component;
- linking to [ayther.dev](https://ayther.dev) or an official repository in the
  [Ayther-Dev GitHub organization](https://github.com/Ayther-Dev);
- reporting news, reviewing the software, publishing commentary, conducting
  research, or discussing the project;
- stating that software is compatible with, integrates with, or is designed for
  AYTHER, when the statement is technically accurate;
- reproducing unmodified screenshots of official AYTHER software for
  documentation, tutorials, reviews, or news coverage;
- using an AYTHER word mark in the title of an issue, article, tutorial, event
  session, or compatibility listing when needed to identify the subject; and
- accurately identifying an unmodified copy of software distributed by the
  AYTHER project.

When practical, use wording such as:

- “compatible with AYTHER”;
- “for AYTHER”;
- “works with AYTHER SDK”; or
- “based on the AYTHER Engine source code.”

The AYTHER name should remain visually secondary to the name and branding of
your own product, service, project, or organization.

## 4. Community projects and modified distributions

The open-source licenses applicable to AYTHER software may allow copying,
modification, and redistribution. Those permissions do not make a modified
version an official AYTHER release.

Without separate written permission:

- a modified distribution must use a distinct primary name;
- it must not present itself as “AYTHER,” an “official AYTHER” product, or an
  official release of an AYTHER component;
- it may describe itself using accurate phrases such as “based on AYTHER” or
  “compatible with AYTHER”;
- it must clearly disclose that it is independently maintained and is not
  affiliated with or endorsed by AYTHER or David Lazarte;
- it must not use the official AYTHER logo, icon, favicon, or a confusingly
  similar visual identity as its primary branding; and
- it must not remove or alter copyright, license, attribution, or trademark
  notices that must be retained under the applicable license.

A suitable notice is:

> This project is independently maintained and is not affiliated with,
> authorized by, endorsed by, or sponsored by AYTHER or David Lazarte.

Permission for one use does not authorize any other use and may be subject to
specific written conditions.

## 6. Prohibited uses

You must not:

- use an AYTHER mark in a false, misleading, deceptive, or confusing manner;
- impersonate AYTHER, David Lazarte, the Ayther-Dev organization, or an official
  AYTHER maintainer;
- suggest sponsorship, endorsement, partnership, certification, employment, or
  affiliation that does not exist;
- use an AYTHER mark for malware, fraud, phishing, credential theft, deceptive
  downloads, or other unlawful or harmful activity;
- use an AYTHER mark to distribute or promote unauthorized ROMs, BIOS files,
  game assets, circumvention tools, or other content that infringes third-party
  rights;
- use a confusingly similar name, logo, domain, icon, or visual identity;
- use an AYTHER mark in a way that damages or misrepresents the reputation of
  AYTHER or identifies unrelated goods or services as originating from AYTHER;
  or
- apply for, register, or claim ownership of an AYTHER mark or any confusingly
  similar trademark, company name, domain name, social-media identifier, or
  other designation.

## 7. Logo and visual-identity use

When written permission to use an official AYTHER logo or icon has been granted,
the asset must:

- be obtained from an official AYTHER source;
- be reproduced without alteration;
- preserve its proportions, colors, clear space, and legibility;
- not be incorporated into another logo or used as a decorative pattern;
- not be placed where it suggests that third-party content or services are
  official; and
- comply with the current AYTHER brand guidelines supplied with the asset.

No permission is granted merely because an official asset is publicly
accessible or included in a source repository.
